This is the list of cockpit 3dbuttons.dat groups.

Refer to the "3Bbuttons_Dev_Notes" for Cockpit groups/list, located in \Data\Art\CkptArt
Use Notepad ++ and Winmerge to identify blocks variants and to make your life easier for the editions of these files.
Notepad++ software (https://notepad-plus-plus.org/) helps in keeping the formatting consistent and help in making researches in files and/or entire folders/sub-folders.
Winmerge software (https://winmerge.org) helps a lot to identify the cockpit button differences.
The files are identical within a group/type of cockpit.
It is important to not break the formatting and respect the boxes. No tabs, Only Spaces.
Each variance of boxes / sections is properly identified.
